About the College

Leading the way in computer science education and innovation

Our Mission

The College of Computer Studies is dedicated to providing world-class education in computer science and technology. We strive to develop competent, ethical, and innovative professionals who can contribute to the advancement of society through technology.

Our Vision

To be a premier institution in computer education, recognized for excellence in teaching, research, and community engagement. We envision producing graduates who are not only technically proficient but also equipped with the critical thinking and problem-solving skills necessary to thrive in an ever-evolving technological landscape.

History

Established in 1995, the College of Computer Studies has grown from a small department to a comprehensive college offering multiple degree programs. Over the years, we have maintained our commitment to academic excellence and innovation, continuously adapting our curriculum to meet the demands of the rapidly changing technology industry.

Core Values

Excellence

We pursue the highest standards in education and research

Innovation

We encourage creativity and forward-thinking approaches

Integrity

We uphold ethical principles in all our endeavors

Collaboration

We foster teamwork and partnerships within and beyond campus

Accreditation

The College of Computer Studies is fully accredited by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and holds Level III accreditation from the Philippine Association of Colleges and Universities Commission on Accreditation (PACUCOA), ensuring that our programs meet the highest standards of quality.
