Academic Programs

Choose your path to success in technology

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

The BS Computer Science program is designed to provide students with a strong foundation in computer science theory, programming, and software development.

This four-year program focuses on the theoretical foundations of information and computation, including algorithm design, software development, computer systems organization, and the mathematical foundations of computing. Students will gain expertise in programming languages, data structures, artificial intelligence, and software engineering.

  • Software Developer/Engineer
  • Systems Analyst
  • Database Administrator
  • AI/Machine Learning Engineer
  • Mobile Application Developer
  • Game Developer
  • Research Scientist
  • Data Structures and Algorithms
  • Object-Oriented Programming
  • Database Management Systems
  • Computer Architecture
  • Operating Systems
  • Software Engineering
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Computer Networks
  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Strong background in Mathematics
  • Passing entrance examination score
  • Interview with program coordinator

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

The BS Information Technology program focuses on the practical application of technology to solve business problems and meet organizational needs.

This program prepares students to work with information technology in various business contexts. Students learn about system administration, network management, web development, and IT project management. The curriculum emphasizes hands-on experience with current technologies and business applications.

  • IT Consultant
  • Network Administrator
  • Web Developer
  • IT Project Manager
  • Systems Administrator
  • Technical Support Specialist
  • IT Security Specialist
  • Web Development
  • Network Fundamentals
  • System Administration
  • IT Project Management
  • Database Administration
  • Cybersecurity Fundamentals
  • Cloud Computing
  • IT Service Management
  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Interest in technology and business
  • Passing entrance examination score
  • Interview with program coordinator

Bachelor of Science in Information Systems

The BS Information Systems program bridges technology and business, preparing students to design and manage information systems that support organizational goals.

This program combines computer science, business, and management principles to prepare students for careers in analyzing business requirements and designing technology solutions. Students learn to integrate technology with business processes to improve organizational efficiency and competitiveness.

  • Business Analyst
  • IS/IT Manager
  • ERP Consultant
  • Data Analyst
  • IT Business Consultant
  • Systems Integration Specialist
  • Digital Transformation Manager
  • Systems Analysis and Design
  • Business Process Management
  • Enterprise Resource Planning
  • Data Analytics
  • Information Systems Strategy
  • Business Intelligence
  • E-Commerce Systems
  • IT Governance
  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Interest in technology and business
  • Passing entrance examination score
  • Interview with program coordinator
